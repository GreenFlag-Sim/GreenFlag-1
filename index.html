01 / THE IDEASIM RACING, EXPLAINED

Real racing skills.
From your own seat.

Sim racing is driving a race car in a video game with a focus on realistic technique. You learn tracks, braking, steering, and race decisions—all in a virtual cockpit.

You don’t need a professional rig to begin. Start with the controller or wheel you have, learn one skill at a time, and upgrade only if it makes sense for you.
